01The problem

Real road footage records faces, plates and daily routes.

Privacy law makes that data slow to collect, reuse and share.

And trained models can leak the data they learned from.

02What you get

Labeled driving data, at the scale you need.

Generated in high-fidelity simulation from the scenarios you choose. No real person appears in any frame.

Every label your perception stack needs.

RGB images, depth, semantic segmentation, class masks and bounding boxes, in standard formats your pipeline already reads.

Your roads. Your weather. Your hours.

Choose environments, traffic, weather and time of day, and add new scenarios as your program grows.

Encrypted and verifiable, file by file.

Strong encryption in transit and at rest, plus a checksum for every file, so you can prove nothing was changed.

03Privacy you can prove

Exposed.

Without protection, an attacker can tell which records a model was trained on.

Guesswork.

Against models trained with MURAGALA protection, the same attack is close to guessing.

Every model gets its own attack test.

Differential privacy protects the statistics behind every scenario, and private training runs under a hard privacy budget that you set.

04Quality you can check

Every delivery ships with a quality report and a data poisoning audit, so you see how the data performs on images from real roads before you train on it.

  • Detection benchmarks on real-world images
  • Image realism scores
  • Faulty and poisoned labels screened out

05Ready for your legal team

  • Signed privacy certificate.
  • Full audit trail.
  • Controls mapped to data protection law.

Starting with GDPR. Each certificate also lists its own limitations, so your team knows exactly what it covers.

08FAQ

Straight answers.

What teams ask before they train on synthetic data.

Ask a question
Is any real person in the data?

No. Every frame is generated in simulation. The real-world statistics used to tune scenarios are protected with differential privacy first.

Can synthetic data replace real data?

Not fully. It reduces how much real data you need to collect and handle. Every delivery includes a quality report, so you can see how it performs on real-world images.

Can you build the scenarios we need?

Yes. Pro and Enterprise include custom environments, traffic, weather and time of day, and we add scenarios as your needs change.

Will it work with our training pipeline?

Data comes in standard image and label formats. Private training works with PyTorch models.

Can we run it on our own infrastructure?

Yes. Enterprise includes on-premise or private-cloud deployment, so data never leaves your environment.

How is privacy measured?

With a privacy budget (ε) that you set, and by attacking every trained model to check that it does not reveal its training data. Results go into your signed certificate.
